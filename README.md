## Hi there, I'm AbdulAziz AbdulGhaffar 👋

I am a Ph.D. Researcher and Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Department of Systems and Computer Engineering at Carleton University (Ottawa, Canada). Working under the supervision of Dr. Ashraf Matrawy, my research bridges theoretical optimization and empirical systems engineering to secure next-generation cellular networks.

### 🔬 Research Focus
* **5G/6G Network Security:** Threat modeling (STRIDE) and securing core network interfaces.
* **Network Slicing Isolation:** Proactive topological isolation and reactive runtime defenses.
* **Programmable Data Planes:** Developing line-rate, closed-loop eBPF/XDP threat mitigation architectures for Linux-based User Plane Functions (UPFs).

### 🛠️ Technical Arsenal
* **5G/Telecom Emulation:** Free5GC, UERANSIM, TRex Traffic Generator
* **Systems & Networking:** Linux Kernel (Ubuntu), Containerlab, Docker, VirtualBox, eBPF, XDP, Socket Programming
* **Languages:** Python, Julia (JuMP/SCIP), Bash, MATLAB, LaTeX, C (eBPF/XDP)
